## Vehicle and Personal Safety

This is an experimental vehicle-mounted monitor accessory. It has **not** been tested or certified for crash loads, emergency stops, vibration durability, heat aging, automotive regulations or compatibility with every vehicle.

A detached mount or monitor could injure an occupant or interfere with vehicle operation. A badly positioned monitor can obstruct the driver's view or interfere with the factory mirror, sensors, trim, wiring, airbags or windshield equipment.

**The installer is responsible** for confirming a safe position, secure retention, unobstructed visibility, no interference with vehicle functions and compliance with local laws. Never adjust or operate the monitor while driving. Do not rely on the accessory as the sole means of observing the roadway.

## Printing and Material Risks

Print integrity varies with filament, orientation, layer adhesion, printer calibration, aging, geometry and use. PETG can creep under stress and soften in a parked vehicle's heat. ASA or other materials may print to different tolerances. Do not assume a printed ball joint or angled neck is load-rated merely because it fits.

Inspect for cracks, delamination, slipping joints and creep before use and periodically afterward. Discontinue use if the assembly moves excessively, loosens, deforms or interferes with the vehicle.
