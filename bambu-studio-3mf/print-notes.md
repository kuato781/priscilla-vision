## Material and Orientation

The proven prototype was printed in **PETG**. The part was positioned **flat in the XY plane of the 90-degree bend**, prioritizing filament continuity through the elbow and angled long arm. Support may be needed around the spheres and overhanging contours.

Use the supplied 3MF as the starting point for exact slicer settings; do **not** assume a particular nozzle, layer height, support-interface spacing or infill percentage without checking the saved project.

The physical print has visible layer lines and potential support-contact marks. This was accepted: **surface finish is not a design requirement**.

## Mechanical Fit

- Kit-side ball: **15.75 mm**
- Voyager-side ball: **20.75 mm**
- Ball sizing may require adjustment for printer calibration, material variation or different manufacturer socket tolerances.
- Do not force a ball through a captive socket lip. Assemble the commercial arm joint as needed.
- Check that the Voyager locking collar can slide over the larger ball's neck **before** ball attachment.

## Heat and Validation

PETG was selected for office printing without the fumes associated with ASA. The current part is a **fit and road-test prototype**, not an automotive-rated component. Short-term winter service does not establish survival in parked-vehicle summer heat. ASA or another suitable higher-temperature production material may be ordered from a printing service after continued fit/vibration checks.

Recheck ball-joint clamping and the entire arm for creep, sagging or fractures. The longer arm can amplify road vibration.
